About SafeVision

Smart system for verifying safety equipment on construction sites

🎯

Objective

Promoting awareness of the importance of safety equipment, assisting in the rapid verification of protective gear, and reducing the likelihood of starting work without essential safety equipment.

🤖

Technologies

  • YOLOv8 (Object Detection)
  • Python + Flask
  • OpenCV + PyTorch
  • HTML5 + CSS3
📊

Training

  • 307 Training Images
  • 57 Validation Images
  • 50 Epochs
  • 17 Classes
⚠️

Important Note

This system is an assistive and awareness tool, and is not a substitute for the safety officer or official safety procedures at the construction site.
